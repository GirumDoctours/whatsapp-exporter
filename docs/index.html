How it works

Three steps, no setup.

It's a small app you start from your computer's command window. You pick a few options with the arrow keys, link your phone once, and it does the rest.

1

Paste one line

Copy the command below into Terminal on a Mac or PowerShell on Windows. It downloads the app and starts it.

2

Pick your options

Quick test or everything, messages only or with media, and whether to hide personal details.

3

Scan the code

Link it like WhatsApp Web, from Linked Devices on your phone. Keep WhatsApp open until the ZIP is saved on your Desktop.

Optional redaction

Share chats without sharing people.

Choose Redact PII and every personal detail is swapped for a clear tag, so the conversation still reads naturally. Names, phone numbers, emails, handles, addresses, ages, ID numbers, bank details and links are covered. ID photos are replaced, documents are replaced, and text in screenshots is blacked out.

Privacy & cost

Your chats stay on your computer.

Exporting is free and happens entirely on your computer. Redaction is optional. It uses Claude, Anthropic's AI, with your own API key, so you pay Anthropic directly and only for what you use.

🖥️

Local by default

The ZIP and its working files live on your computer. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose redaction or voice-note transcripts.

🛡️

Details hidden first

When redacting, known names, numbers and emails are hidden on your computer before chat text goes to Claude. Photos are checked on your computer; in rare borderline cases the text read from a photo is checked too.

🧹

Cleans up after itself

When it's done it unlinks from your phone and deletes its working files, so only the ZIP is left. If you redact, the original isn't kept.

Questions

Good to know.

Is this made by WhatsApp?

No. It's an unofficial, independent tool, not made by or affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta. It links to your account as a linked device, the same way WhatsApp Web does. Unofficial clients are against WhatsApp's terms of service. Read-only use like this is low risk, but not zero.

Will it export everything I've ever sent?

It exports what your phone still has. Deleted messages, media you've cleared, or history lost in a reinstall without a backup can't be recovered. Older photos have to be re-sent by your phone, so they take longer, and anything the phone no longer has is listed in the report.

What if it stops partway?

Paste the same line again and choose to continue. It remembers what it was doing and picks up where it left off. If your phone locks, it pauses and carries on by itself when you open WhatsApp again.

Is redaction perfect?

It's careful but not perfect. It combines local checks with several AI passes and a strict final check, but you should still look over the redacted copy before sharing it. Videos aren't checked and are copied as they are.

Do I need an API key?

Only for AI redaction. Create one at platform.claude.com under Settings, then API keys, and paste it when asked. Without a key, redaction uses local checks only, which miss more. Voice-note transcripts need a separate Deepgram key, also optional.

How do I remove it from my phone afterwards?

It unlinks itself when it finishes. To double-check, open Settings → Linked Devices on your phone and make sure WhatsApp Exporter isn't listed.
